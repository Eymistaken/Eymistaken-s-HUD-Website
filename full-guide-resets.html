When something is off

What each reset resets

Five buttons say some form of reset, and no two of them mean the same thing. It is the most common source of surprise, so it gets a page of its own.

The five resets

ButtonWhereWhat it touches
Reset HUD LayoutThe General page's Editor tab, and the editor's Reset HUDAnchors, offsets and scales only. Colors and behavior are untouched, and Prevent Overlap goes back on.
Reset SettingsA module's right-click menuThat one module's visual settings, and nothing else. On Combo it covers every setting except the anchor, the offsets and the on and off switch, so Scale % and the Behavior tab reset too. Your best combo does not.
Reset BestThe Combo page's Behavior tabOnly your best combo. It asks first, and Cancel cannot bring it back, since the record is not part of the config.
Reset LayoutThe Designer's toolbarThe stock nine keys, and the design, animation, palette and every per-key override.
Reset All SettingsThe settings screenEverything. Saved presets, third-party module data and your best combo are kept. It cannot be undone.

Reset Position, the first row of every module's right-click menu, is the narrowest of all: it puts back that module's anchor, offsets and scale, and leaves whether it is switched on alone.

Third-party module settings survive a full reset on purpose. Those modules provide their own Reset Settings row instead.
