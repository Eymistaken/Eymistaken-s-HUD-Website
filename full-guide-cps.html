Modules

CPS

Clicks per second for the left and right mouse buttons, counted over a rolling 1 second window. It is the only module that is on by default.

On by default. Three tabs: Style, Background, Text. In the editor's right-click menu: Reset Settings, Enable CPS, Text Color, Scale %.

Style

SettingDefaultRangeWhat it does
Right Click CPSOnShows the right-click count beside the left.
Text ColorWhiteColor of the numbers.
RainbowOffCycles the text through every hue. Overrides Text Color.

Background

SettingDefaultRangeWhat it does
Show BackgroundOffDraws a box behind the text.
Background ColorBlackColor of the box.
Background Opacity1280–2550 is invisible, 255 is solid.

Text

SettingDefaultRangeWhat it does
Left Prefixempty64 charsText before the left-click count.
Separator" | "64 charsText between the two counts.
Right Prefixempty64 charsText before the right-click count.

The three fields write the whole readout. The defaults give 12 | 4, and a Left Prefix of CPS: gives CPS: 12 | 4.
