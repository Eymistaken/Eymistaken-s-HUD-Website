A competitive HUD you place yourself.

Seven PvP modules, an in-game editor that snaps them into place, and a whole layout you can hand to a friend as one line of text.

Download 1.2.5 Read the User Guide

For Minecraft 26.3 on Fabric, and it runs on your client only. Writing an addon? The API quick start gets a module on screen in three files.

Seven modules

Each carries its own color, scale, background and anchor. CPS is on from the start; switch on the rest as you need them.

Keystrokes

Not a fixed WASD block. Add any key or mouse button, resize and recolor each one, and put a CPS counter on any key you like. Press animations stack: tick as many as you want.

Eleven designs to start from, from the plain Baseline and Frame to Neon, Hex and Pixel Bevel. Wedge and Hex bring their own arrangement of the keys.

The Keystrokes Designer

Where the keystrokes layout is drawn. Keys snap to their neighbors and to even gaps, every key has its own inspector, and undo covers the design and colors as well as the keys. Open it by right-clicking Keystrokes in the editor.

Combo

Counts a hit once the server confirms it, shows a combo landed on you as a negative line, and remembers your best. Colors climb through heatmap tiers at 5, 8 and 12 hits by default.

Armor HUD

Four armor slots, main hand and off hand, with durability bars and exact numbers. Items shake when they take a hit and show a red ! before they break. Every slot can be placed on its own, even with nothing equipped.

The readouts

0:16

CPS

Left and right clicks over the last second. Write it as 12 | 4, CPS: 12 or anything else.

0:13

Reach

The distance of your last hit in blocks, to 2 decimal places. It can count players only, and show a label of your own before the first hit.

0:23

FPS

Frames per second, updated every tick, for when you want the number without opening F3.

Ping

Your latency in milliseconds: the same value the tab list shows, refreshed with every server update.

Right Shift

The editor is the HUD.

The editor draws over your live HUD, so what you drag is the real thing at its real size. Line an edge up with another module and a magenta guide appears; let go and it glides into place.

Select two or more and a toolbar aligns or distributes them. A 4, 8, 16 or 32 px grid is a right-click away.

Layouts that travel.

Presets

Server configs

The whole HUD, or just the keystrokes look. A code is checked before anything changes, and your current setup is backed up first.

For mod developers

Build your own module.

Register a module through the eymistaken_hud entrypoint and it gets a place in the editor, a generated page in the settings screen, and, when you opt in, a seat in presets, server configs and share codes.

Every hook added since the API first shipped has a default, so an addon built against an older release keeps running on a newer one.
