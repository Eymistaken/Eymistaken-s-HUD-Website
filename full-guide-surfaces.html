Basics

The four surfaces

There are four places settings can be changed, and several settings live on exactly one of them. This is the page to check when you cannot find something.

Where each one opens

In-world HUD editorPress Right Shift in game. It can be rebound under Controls, in a category called Eymistaken's HUD, and it only opens when no other screen is up.
Settings screenFrom the editor's Settings row, or from Mod Menu.
Keystrokes DesignerKeystrokes page → Layout → Open Designer, or right-click the Keystrokes module in the editor.
Mod MenuOptional. When it is not installed, the settings screen is still reachable through the editor.

Settings that exist in only one place

The Designer's animation drawerWhich motions and fills are switched on. They are a set, several running at once, and a control that shows one value at a time cannot express a set, so no other screen offers them.
The Designer's inspectorEverything about a single key: its colors, its own design and animation, bold, italic, underline and shadow, label placement and size, line mode, Hidden and Show CPS.
The in-world editorThe twelve per-slot armor offsets, Group and Ungroup, the align and distribute toolbar, cycling the grid size, and managing per-server configs.
The settings screenThe settings search, each row's default and its reset, the live preview, Reset All Settings, Cancel, Combo's Reset Best button, the preset card and the typed share-code field.
