Modules

Combo

Counts consecutive hits. A hit counts once the server confirms it landed, and a combo someone lands on you is counted too, shown as a negative number on a line of its own. This is the module with the most to tune, because what counts as a combo genuinely differs between servers.

Off by default. Four tabs: Behavior, Style, Animation, Background. In the editor's right-click menu: Reset Settings, Enable Combo, Always Show, Color Mode, Style, Text Color, Format.

What counts as a hit

Hit Detection decides when a hit is counted, Combat Mode whether it was strong enough, and Only Players what it has to land on.

ServerThe default. Attacking records a pending hit, which counts when the server sends the damage event for that target. The mod waits roughly 200 ms plus your ping, never longer than a second. Hits into invulnerability frames, hits blocked by a shield, arrows and sweep damage do not add to the combo.
ClientThe old behavior: every attack you send counts.
Combat ModeApplies under either detection mode. Modern counts only hits made with the cooldown at least 90% recharged; Classic counts every hit.
Only PlayersOn by default, so only hits on players count. Off, mobs count too.

Servers that do not report attackers. Some servers never say who dealt the damage, older versions joined through a protocol translator for instance. On those, the hit you just sent is taken as the cause, and damage you take is attributed to whatever last swung within 6 blocks. As soon as the server reports an attacker once, that guesswork switches off.

When a combo ends

TimeoutNo counted hit for as long as the Timeout, and the combo ends, or starts draining if Decay is on.
DistanceA target more than 20 blocks away counts as a timeout. This check always applies.
Target switchWith Continue On Target Switch off, hitting a different target ends the combo and starts a new one.
FinisherA target that dies within a second of your last hit plays the finisher moment. With Continue On Target Switch off the combo ends there; with it on, it carries on.
DamageWith Break on Any Damage on, any damage you take breaks the combo. Off, only damage from someone you have landed a counted hit on within the Timeout does. Thorns never breaks it.
World changeChanging world or dimension resets the tracking.

A combo that ends on a timeout, the distance check or a finisher, at Min Display or above, lingers with its final count for 1.2 seconds: full brightness for 0.3 seconds, then down to 45% opacity over 0.25. A new combo, yours or one landed on you, takes over right away.

A combo landed on you

Hits the same attacker lands on you in a row are counted and shown as a negative number: the default format reads -5 Combo.

What countsHits from players, and from mobs too if Only Players is off. Melee and projectiles both count, arrows included. Thorns and environmental damage do not.
New attackerA hit from a different attacker starts the count again from 1.
When it endsWhen you land a counted hit on that attacker, when the attacker dies, or when the Timeout passes after their last hit.

With a combo of your own at the same time, the two stack: yours on top, a 1 px divider, the negative line under it. Stacking needs Decay on or Break on Any Damage off. Its space is reserved up front, so modules on the same edge do not jump when you are hit. Its color deepens from yellow through orange and red to nearly black, whatever the Color Mode.

How hits land

Every counted hit punches the number up and lets it settle with a slight bounce. On the big moments the counter also freezes for an instant, like hitstop in a fighting game. No freeze lasts longer than 160 ms. Figures are at Impact 100%.

MomentPunchFreeze
A normal hit8%, settles in 140 msnone
A new tier18%60 ms
Each hit past tier 310%, rising to 16%30 ms + 3 ms per hit, up to 90 ms
A new best22%90 ms
A finisher20% + 1% per hit, up to 40%70 ms + 4 ms per hit, up to 160 ms
A broken combo, tier 1 or higher, Decay offnone50 ms + 3 ms per hit, up to 120 ms

Your count starts to shake at tier 3, by 1 px, and builds to 3 px over as many hits as separate tier 2 from tier 3, four on Medium.

Your best combo

Kept between sessions in simplecps_stats.json, beside simplecps.json in your config folder, as {"bestCombo": N}. It is not part of the config, so presets, server configs and share codes do not carry it, and neither Reset Settings nor Reset All Settings clears it.

A new best plays once per combo, when it passes the record and reaches at least tier 2. The number turns gold (#FFB800) for the freeze, and a small gold BEST label appears beside it for 1.6 seconds, on the side away from the anchor.

The line under the count

TimeoutThe default. A bar in the count's color at 75% opacity that shortens with the time the combo has left and refills on every counted hit.
HitsYour last 8 counted hits, one segment each: gray for a normal hit, white for a sprint hit, gold for a critical one. The newest sits at the right end.
BothThe timeout bar on top, the hit strip 1 px below it.
OffNo line.

Room is reserved. The line takes 2 px, or 4 px with Both, reserved up front so modules on the same edge do not jump as it comes and goes. Because the line is on by default, those modules move down 2 px once after updating.

Format and Style

You write the whole readout, up to 64 characters, with two placeholders. Leave {combo} out and the count is added at the front, so editing the format can never lose the number.

FormatReads
{combo} Combo12 Combo
x{combo}x12
{combo} hits (best {best})12 hits (best 30)
Hits12 Hits

Big draws the count twice as large with the rest of the format under it: {combo} Combo becomes a big 12 over Combo. Show Background with a Line gives a badge look, and x{combo} a minimal one.

The preview and the editor

On the Combo page, the settings screen's preview plays a looping demo fight: the count climbs through the tiers, goes three hits past tier 3 and ends on a finisher, with sprint and critical hits along the way. Every slider can be tried without joining a world.

The Right Shift editor shows a still sample instead: 8 Combo on Medium, with -5 Combo under it when stacking is possible.
