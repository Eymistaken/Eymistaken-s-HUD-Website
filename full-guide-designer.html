Keystrokes in depth

The Keystrokes Designer

A free canvas where you place, resize, rename and restyle every key. It draws real designs rather than neutral editor boxes, so what you arrange is what the HUD will actually render.

Open it from the Keystrokes page's Layout tab, or right-click Keystrokes in the editor and choose Open Designer.

Toolbar

Undo and RedoSixty steps. Each step is a snapshot of the whole look, not just the key list: picking a design rewrites your colors too, and an undo that put the keys back while leaving the colors changed would be worse than none.
SnapSnapping while you drag. On by default, and not saved: it belongs to the session, not to your layout.
Reset LayoutRestores the stock nine keys and the shipping design, animation, palette and per-key overrides. Putting back only the positions would leave the previous design painted over a default grid, which does not read as a reset.
ImportPaste a keystrokes share code with Ctrl V.
ExportCopies your whole keystrokes look to the clipboard.
DesignThe gallery: all eleven designs on screen at once. Each card previews the stock keys in miniature over a backdrop that stands in for the world behind the HUD, and animates itself, because a design cannot be judged from its resting state alone. The footer names the design, says in a line what it is, ending in Own layout if it will move your keys, and tags the press animation it ships with. The design in use is marked IN USE.
AnimThe animation drawer, the only place motions and fills are ticked.

The left rail

Add elementClick to place a Key (21 × 21), a Modifier (33 × 13), Mouse L or Mouse R (33 × 21), or a Space Bar (67 × 13).
LayersOne row per key. The dot hides and shows a key; click to select it, and hold Ctrl while clicking to add to the selection.
PresetsWASD is the stock nine keys. Full adds Q, E and both mouse buttons. Mouse is the two mouse buttons on their own.

The canvas shows a safe area of 400 × 300 pixels: anything laid out inside it fits on screen at any sensible scale.

The canvas ground is a mid gray by default, so the stock translucent-black keys and the gaps between them stay visible. The BG switch above the canvas cycles gray, light and dark for designs that are themselves very light or very dark. A selected key is tinted blue with a 1 px edge on its own outline, which leaves the gaps to its neighbors in view.

The inspector: per-key settings

Select a key and every one of these becomes available, grouped under Key, Box, Label, Color, Design and Behavior. None of them exist on any other screen.

SettingDefaultRangeWhat it does
KeybindPress a key to bind it; Esc cancels. Binding also renames the label. Mouse buttons read as Mouse L, Mouse R or Mouse followed by a number.
Label64 charsThe text drawn on the key.
Position X and Y±10000Where the key sits in the cluster.
Size W and H1–1024Width and height of the key.
ContentTextText, LineLine draws a solid bar instead of the label, which is what a space bar actually wants.
PlacementCenteredWhere the label sits inside the key. Centered until you drag it; the Center button puts it back.
Text size100%50–400Label size. In line mode the row is Thickness, and sets the thickness of the bar.
Length100%10–100Bar length as a share of the key's inner width. Only shown in line mode.
StyleShadow onB, I, U, SBold, italic, underline and shadow. In line mode only the shadow applies.
Normal colorGlobalOverrides the module color for this key. Use Global puts it back.
Pressed colorGlobalThe same, for the held state.
DesignGlobal10 designsGives this one key a different look. Timeline is not offered, since it is global only.
Motion and FillGlobalOne animation for this key, replacing the module's whole set.
DirectionGlobalOnly appears when the active fill actually reads a direction.
Show CPSOffDraws a live click counter on the button. Mouse keys only.
AnimationOnSwitches every animation off for this key alone.
