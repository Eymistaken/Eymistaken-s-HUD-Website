Reach

The distance of your last landed hit in blocks, measured at the moment of the hit and shown to 2 decimal places.

Off by default. Three tabs: Behavior, Style, Background. In the editor's right-click menu: Reset Settings, Enable Reach, Always Show, Text Color, No Hit Text.

Behavior

SettingDefaultRangeWhat it does
Timeout3 s1–10 sHow long the last measurement stays on screen.
Only PlayersOnOnly measures hits on players.
Always ShowOffKeeps the display up while idle, showing the No Hit text.
No Hit TextNo Hit64 charsShown before your first hit, and while idle if Always Show is on.

Style

RainbowOffCycles the text through every hue. Overrides Text Color.
