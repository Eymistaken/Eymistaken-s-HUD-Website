Modules

Armor HUD

Your four armor pieces and your held items, with durability. It is the only module that starts in the bottom left rather than the top left.

Off by default. Three tabs: Layout, Style, Effects. In the editor's right-click menu: Reset Settings, Ungroup, Enable Armor, Scale %, Vertical, Show Background, Durability Text, Damage Effects, Show Main Hand, Show Off Hand. The effect sliders are only in the settings screen.

Layout

SettingDefaultWhat it does
Vertical OrientationOnStacks the items in a column. Off lays them in a row.
Show Main HandOnIncludes the item you are holding.
Show Off HandOffIncludes your off-hand item.

Style

SettingDefaultWhat it does
Show Background SlotsOffDraws a semi-transparent inventory slot behind each item.
Durability TextOffPrints the exact number next to each item instead of relying on the bar.

No colors and no background box. Armor draws items, not text, so there is nothing to tint; the slot backgrounds are the equivalent switch.

Hit and wear effects

The Effects tab decides how items react. Every effect is its own slider from 0 to 200% of its stock size, where 0 turns it off, and any number of them can run at once. Every motion is timed rather than random, so the same hit always plays the same way, and the preview card takes a pretend hit every 2 seconds while you are on the tab.

SettingDefaultRangeWhat it does
Damage EffectsOnPlays the four hit effects below when an item loses durability. Off silences all four, whatever their sliders say. This was Damage Flash before 1.2.4, and it keeps your old choice.
Hit Shake %1000–200Knocks the hit item up and over for an instant, then lets it settle with a shake that dies away.
Hit Sparks %00–200Throws red sparks out of the hit item. The slider sets how far they travel.
Warning Corners %00–200Blinks red brackets around the slot twice, closing in on the second blink. The slider sets their length.
Chip Damage %00–200Keeps the durability the hit took on the bar in white for a moment before it drains away, so even a small loss shows. The slider sets how long.
Low Durability At %255–50The durability left under which the four warnings below start. They grow stronger and faster as the item nears breaking.
Warning Badge %1000–200A small red ! on the item's corner that hops and wiggles now and then. The slider sets how much.
Tremble %00–200The item grows a little and shivers in bursts that come closer together.
Heartbeat %00–200The item beats in a double pulse whose tempo climbs from 60 to 150 a minute.
Red Pulse %00–200A red glow breathes behind the item.

The warnings only apply to items that can wear out. A hit is a drop in durability on the same item: switching to a more worn one, such as a second sword on your hotbar, does not count.

Splitting the slots apart

Right-click the Armor HUD in the editor and choose Ungroup. The six pieces, Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, Boots, Main Hand and Off Hand, become independently draggable, each with its own offset, so your boots can sit somewhere entirely different from your helmet.

Right-click any detached slot and choose Group to snap everything back into the stack and clear all twelve offsets at once.

Empty slots are not drawn in game, so the editor stands in for them. With nothing equipped in the group, every slot shows as a ghost: a dark, sunken slot holding a gray copy of the diamond piece that goes there, or the empty off-hand silhouette, so there is still something to drag. As soon as the group holds anything only the real items show, because that is what your HUD will look like. A detached slot keeps its ghost whenever it is empty. The ghosts are made from your resource pack's textures.

Slot offsets live only in the editor. There are no rows for the twelve per-slot offsets in the settings screen: dragging is the interface.
